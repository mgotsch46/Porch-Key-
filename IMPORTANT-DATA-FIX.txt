WHY SETUP KEPT ASKING — and the bigger problem behind it
=========================================================

Short version: your database was being erased on every deploy. Setup reappeared
because the app genuinely was seeing a brand-new install each time.


WHAT WENT WRONG
---------------
Your app stores everything — the SQLite database and every uploaded document — in
a folder called /data. On Railway, a container's own disk is temporary; anything
written there disappears when the app redeploys or restarts. Persisting data needs
a Volume mounted at that path.

You had one. When I consolidated two services earlier, I asked Railway's agent to
move the volume to the surviving service and delete the empty one. It deleted the
old service and the volume went with it. The new service was left with DATA_DIR=/data
pointing at temporary storage, and I did not verify the mount afterwards.

That was my error, and it is the kind that only shows up later — which is exactly
what happened.


WHAT I FIXED
------------
  - Created a new volume and mounted it at /data on the porchpay service
  - Committed it and confirmed the service now reports hasVolume: true
  - Deploy completed successfully

From this point your loans, buyers, documents and messages survive redeploys.


WHAT WAS LOST
-------------
Anything you entered before now. If you created test properties or buyers while
trying the app out, they are gone. Nothing you had entered was real production
data as far as I know — but check, and re-enter anything that mattered.

The good news on timing: this surfaced before you had live buyers in the system.


ALSO FIXED — setup can no longer repeat
---------------------------------------
Two safeguards, independent of the volume:

  1. If a company already has a real name and you have set your own password, the
     app now treats setup as finished even if the flag went missing. You cannot be
     walked through it twice.

  2. The wizard has a "Skip — I'll do this later" button. It dismisses permanently.

So even if something else goes sideways in future, setup will not nag you.

------------------------------------

Worth doing after any change to the service.


BACKUPS — worth setting up before real buyers
---------------------------------------------
A volume protects against redeploys. It does not protect against a mistaken delete.
Everything lives in one file, so a backup is quick:

    npm i -g @railway/cli
    railway login
    railway link
    railway run cp -r /data ./backup-%date%

Do this before any significant change, and on a schedule once you have live loans.
