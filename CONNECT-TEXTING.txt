CONNECTING TEXTING — why invitations aren't sending, and how to fix it
=====================================================================

The app has always been built to text the invitation. It has never been able to,
because there is no Twilio account connected. I checked your Railway variables —
there are no Twilio credentials there. So every invitation fell back to "copy this
and send it from your phone".

That is the only thing standing between you and automatic texting.


WHAT I CHANGED
--------------
1. THE SALE TEXTS THE BUYER BY ITSELF.
   Recording a sale and inviting the buyer are now one action. The moment you save
   the sale, the text goes out. No second screen, no button. You get a confirmation
   saying it was texted and to which number.

   If it cannot send, it says exactly why — no Twilio, no mobile number, bad number,
   carrier rejection — rather than quietly doing nothing.

2. YOU CAN CONNECT TWILIO FROM INSIDE THE APP.
   Settings → Texting. Previously this meant editing server variables in Railway.
   Now you paste three values into the app and it checks them with Twilio before
   saving, so a typo tells you immediately instead of failing silently on a real
   buyer weeks later.

3. TWILIO'S ERROR CODES ARE TRANSLATED.
   Instead of "Twilio error 21608" you get "Your Twilio trial can only text verified
   numbers — upgrade the account or verify this number first." The half dozen errors
   that actually happen in practice are all covered.

4. A TEST BUTTON. Once connected, send yourself a text to prove the whole path works
   before a real buyer depends on it.


WHAT YOU NEED TO DO — about fifteen minutes
-------------------------------------------
1. Sign up at twilio.com. A card is required. Texts cost roughly a penny each, so a
   handful of buyers is a couple of dollars a month.

2. Buy a phone number with SMS enabled. Choose your own area code — people answer
   local numbers.

3. From the Twilio console home page, copy the Account SID and the Auth Token.

4. In Porch Pay: Settings → Texting. Paste the Account SID, Auth Token and your new
   number. Press Connect. It verifies with Twilio before saving.

5. Send yourself a test text.

6. In Twilio, open your number → Messaging → "A message comes in" and set it to:

       POST  https://porchpay-production.up.railway.app/sms/incoming

   This is what makes vendor replies come back into the app. The exact URL is shown
   on the Settings → Texting screen once you are connected, so you can copy it there.

7. Start A2P 10DLC registration in Twilio the same day. US carriers filter
   unregistered business texting, and approval takes a few days. Do it before you
   have live buyers depending on it.

I cannot do steps 1 through 3 for you — creating an account and entering payment
details is yours to do. Everything after that is paste-and-press.


WHILE IT IS NOT CONNECTED
-------------------------
Nothing is blocked. The invitation is still created, the buyer login still works, and
you can still copy the message and send it from your own phone. The app just says
plainly that it could not send rather than pretending.
