TB LOANS vs PML LOANS
---------------------
You said these looked like duplicates. They are opposite sides of the same house, so
they are now labelled to make that obvious rather than merged:

  TB Loans   what a tenant buyer owes you. The ledger, posting payments, the
             amortization schedule, notices, documents, messaging the buyer.
  PML Loans  what you owe a private lender on that same property. Payments out,
             balloon dates, lien position, and the spread between the two.

Both pages say so at the top now, and each points at the other.


SEARCH ON BOTH
--------------
TB Loans: search buyer name, address, city, buyer email or loan number. Filter by
Active / Past due only / Current only / Paid off. Every column header sorts — click
once for ascending, again for descending. The count line tells you how many are past
due. Click any line to open the loan.

PML Loans: search lender name, property or lender contact, with a status filter.

Same search already on Properties: address, city, ZIP, county, trust, trustee, owner
and buyer name.


ON A PHONE
----------
The bottom bar is five tabs: Dashboard, Properties, Messages, Calculator, More.
Everything else is in More, grouped under the same headings as the sidebar.

Which buttons appear in the phone bar is now decided by a marker on each button rather
than by their position in the list. Previously it was "the first five", which meant
reordering the sidebar silently changed the phone. It cannot any more.


A BUG THIS TURNED UP
--------------------
While reorganising I found a broken CSS rule. An earlier edit had accidentally matched
part of the rule that hides the "More" button on desktop, which destroyed it and also
mis-scoped the styling for action-button rows. Both repaired. You would have seen a
stray "More" button on the desktop sidebar and slightly wrong button spacing.
