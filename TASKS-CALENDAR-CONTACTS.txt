TASKS, CALENDAR, CONTACTS AND NOTES — what was built
====================================================


TASKS
-----
A task can be tied to a property or stand on its own. Both work; the property is
optional on every task.

Each task has: a title, a type (boots on the ground, rehab, legal, filing, closing,
insurance, taxes, showing, inspection, collections, general), a priority, a due date
and optional time, notes, and who it is assigned to if you have staff.

The list groups itself the way you actually work: Overdue, Today, Next 7 days, Later,
No date, then Done. Four counters sit across the top. Overdue turns red and puts a
badge on the Tasks tab.

REPEATING
Weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly, quarterly or yearly, with an optional stop date. Tick
one off and the next one is created automatically — you never have to remember to
re-create it. Month-end arithmetic is handled properly: a task on the 31st repeating
monthly lands on the 28th in February, not in March.

REMINDERS
Any task can pop up a notification a set number of days ahead — on the day, 1, 3, 7,
14 or 30 days before. Same delivery as the payment reminders you already have.

WHERE TO FIND THEM
Tasks page (nav), the calendar, and a Tasks card on each property showing just that
property's work with the completed ones tucked into a fold-out.


CALENDAR
--------
Month grid on a desktop, a plain agenda list on a phone — the grid is unreadable at
that width, so it switches automatically.

Four layers, each with its own colour and its own on/off switch:

  green   Your tasks
  blue    Payments due in from buyers, with the amount
  purple  Payments out to your private money lenders, plus balloon dates
  amber   Insurance expiry and property tax due dates

Switch all three automatic layers off and you are left with only what you put there.
Anything past due shows red on both the grid and the list.

Click any day to add a task on that date. Each row in the agenda has a button into the
thing it refers to — the loan, the lender, the property, the task.

The insurance and tax dates come from the property. Open a property → Owner & payment
terms → Renewal dates. Fill those in once and they appear every year.


CONTACTS
--------
Your people, kept once and attached to whichever houses they work on: boots on the
ground, contractors, attorneys, title, insurance agents, lenders, inspectors, agents,
accountants, utilities.

Each contact holds a name, role, business name, mobile, email, address and notes. Add
one from the Contacts page, or straight from a property — creating it there attaches it
in the same step.

On every property there is now a "Who works on this house" card listing the people
attached, with a Text button beside each.


TEXTING VENDORS — and how it differs from buyer texting
-------------------------------------------------------
These are deliberately different, and the Messages page now says so plainly with two
tabs: "In-app — buyers" and "Text — vendors".

  BUYERS get exactly one text, ever: the invitation to download the app. It goes from
  a send-only number, says it is unmonitored, and carries the STOP opt-out carriers
  require. If they reply, they get an automatic answer pointing them into the app.
  Everything after that happens in-app, where it is recorded against the loan and
  costs nothing per message.

  VENDORS get real two-way SMS. You text them, they text back, and their reply lands
  in the thread in the app with a notification to you. Carrier charges apply per
  message.

Both use the same Twilio number. The app decides which behaviour applies by looking up
the incoming number: known contact → route to the thread, anyone else → auto-reply.

Every vendor text is signed automatically with your name and company, and if you pick a
property the address goes at the top, so nobody has to ask "which house?".

TEXT SEVERAL AT ONCE
Where a property has more than one contact with a mobile, there is a "Text several at
once" button. One message, sent individually — they never see each other's numbers.
Failures are reported per person rather than silently swallowed.

WITHOUT TWILIO
Nothing is blocked. Write the message and you get the exact text back to copy and send
from your own phone, and it is still recorded in the thread marked "not sent".


NOTES
-----
A free-form notes section under the history on both properties and loans. The phone
call, the handshake, the lockbox code, the thing you will not remember in March.

Each note records who wrote it and when, and marks itself edited if changed. Pin the
important ones and they float to the top. Internal only — a buyer never sees a note.


THE DASHBOARD
-------------
A strip across the top now surfaces anything wanting you: overdue tasks, tasks due
today, new vendor texts, unread buyer messages, invitations waiting to go out. Each one
is a button straight into the right place. It disappears when there is nothing.

This matters on a phone, where the sidebar badges are hidden behind More.


ONE SETUP STEP, WHEN YOU OPEN TWILIO
------------------------------------
In the phone number settings, set "A message comes in" to:

    POST  https://porchpay-production.up.railway.app/sms/incoming

Without it, vendor replies vanish silently. Everything else works either way.

Also worth knowing: business texting in the US requires A2P 10DLC registration through
Twilio. It takes a few days and unregistered traffic gets filtered by the carriers, so
start it early.
