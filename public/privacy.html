Who we are

Porch Pay is a loan servicing app. Your loan is serviced by the company that gave you your login (your "Servicer"). The Servicer decides what data is collected about your loan and is the party responsible for it. Porch Pay provides the software the Servicer uses.

Payment information is handled by our processors, not by us

When you pay by card, bank transfer, or Cash App Pay, your payment credentials go directly to Stripe. When you pay cash at a retail store, your payment is handled by PayNearMe. We never see or store your full card number or bank account number. We receive only a confirmation that the payment was made and the amount. See Stripe's privacy policy and PayNearMe's privacy policy.

Location sharing is optional and off by default

You do not have to share your location to use Porch Pay. Every feature — viewing your loan, making payments, messaging, and reading documents — works with location turned off.

If you choose to turn it on, we collect your approximate device location when you open the app and share it with your Servicer for account verification and delivery of documents. We keep at most your 50 most recent locations. We do not track you in the background, we do not sell location data, and we do not use it for advertising. You can turn it off at any time in the app under Loan → Location sharing, which immediately deletes your stored location history.

Messaging and electronic notices

By accepting these terms you agree to receive communications about your loan through the app, including account notices, late notices, and other notices about your loan. The app records when a notice was sent and when you opened it. Electronic delivery in the app may be provided in addition to — not instead of — any notice your Servicer is required by law or by your contract to send by mail or other means.

Who we share information with

  • Your Servicer — the company servicing your loan sees all of your loan and account information.
  • Payment processors — Stripe and PayNearMe, to process payments you initiate.
  • Service providers — hosting and infrastructure providers that operate the app on our behalf.
  • Legal — when required by law, subpoena, or to protect legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

How long we keep your information

Loan and payment records are kept as long as required by law and by the terms of your loan, which is typically several years after the loan ends. Messages and notices are kept for the life of the loan. Location history is deleted as soon as you turn location sharing off.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export — download a copy of your data at any time from Settings → My data in the app.
  • Delete your account — you can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete account, or at porchpay/delete-account. This erases your login, contact information, consent records and location history. Loan ledger entries are retained in de-identified form where the law requires the Servicer to keep lending records.
  • Correct your information — message your Servicer in the app.
  • Withdraw location consent — turn it off in the app at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas), you may have additional rights to know, correct, delete, or limit use of your personal information. Contact your Servicer or use the app's export and delete tools to exercise them.

Security

Passwords are stored using salted cryptographic hashing, connections are encrypted in transit, and each Servicer's data is isolated so no company can access another's records. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and tell your Servicer immediately if you think your account has been accessed.

Children

Porch Pay is not directed to children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the version above and ask you to accept the new version the next time you open the app.

Contact

Questions about your loan or your data: message your Servicer in the app, or see Support.
