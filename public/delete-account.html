Delete your Porch Pay account

This page satisfies Google Play's requirement for a web-accessible account deletion route. You can also delete your account inside the app under Settings → Delete account.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and password
  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your saved payment methods
  • Your consent records
  • The text of messages you sent

What is kept, and why

Your loan's payment ledger is retained in de-identified form. Lending and loan servicing records carry legal retention requirements, so your Servicer must keep a record of what was paid and when. Deleting your app account does not cancel your loan or change what you owe. Your obligations under your contract continue.
