Messages

In-app messaging. Secure, threaded conversations with your tenant buyers inside the PorchPay app. Nothing leaves the app, so there are no carrier fees, no message limits, and the whole history is kept against the loan. This is where late notices, receipts and templates go.

Text messaging. Real SMS out to the people who work on your houses — boots on the ground, contractors, attorneys, title, insurance. Two-way: their replies come back in here and you get a notification. Carrier charges apply per message.

Buyers are deliberately not in this list. They get exactly one text — the invitation to download the app — from a send-only number, and everything after that happens in-app where it is recorded against the loan.

Enter to send · Shift+Enter for a new line
