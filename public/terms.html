1. What Porch Pay is

Porch Pay is an app for viewing your loan, making payments, exchanging messages with the company servicing your loan (your "Servicer"), and accessing your documents. Porch Pay is software. Porch Pay is not your lender and does not own your loan. Your loan is governed entirely by the contract you signed with your Servicer — your land contract, contract for deed, trust agreement, or similar document. If anything in the app conflicts with that contract, the contract controls.

2. Your account

Your Servicer creates your account and gives you a temporary password. You must set your own password when you first sign in. Keep it confidential — you are responsible for activity under your account. Tell your Servicer right away if you believe someone else has access. Your account shows only your own loan.

3. Payments

  • Payments made in the app are processed by Stripe (card, bank transfer, Cash App Pay) or PayNearMe (cash at participating retail stores). Their terms apply to the payment transaction.
  • A payment is credited to your loan when the processor confirms it, not when you submit it. Bank transfers and retail cash payments can take several business days.
  • Third parties may charge a fee for cash payments at a retail store. That fee is disclosed before you pay and is not a payment on your loan.
  • Payments are applied in the order set by your contract. In this app the default order is: fees, then accrued interest, then escrow, then principal. Extra amounts you designate as extra principal are applied to principal.
  • Figures shown in the app — including balances, amortization schedules and payoff estimates — are provided for your convenience. A payoff estimate is not an official payoff letter. Request one from your Servicer.
  • Failed, returned, or reversed payments may result in fees under your contract.

4. Electronic communications and notices

By accepting these terms you consent to receive communications and notices about your loan electronically through the app, including account messages, payment reminders, late notices, and other notices about your loan. The app records when a notice is sent and when you open it.

Electronic notice does not replace notice required by law. Where your contract or applicable law requires a notice to be delivered by mail, personal service, or another specific method, your Servicer must still deliver it that way. You may withdraw consent to electronic communications by contacting your Servicer, though doing so may mean you can no longer use the app.

5. Location sharing is optional

Location sharing is off by default and is never required to use Porch Pay. If you turn it on, your approximate device location is shared with your Servicer for account verification and delivery of documents. You may turn it off at any time in the app, which deletes your stored location history.

6. Acceptable use

Do not use Porch Pay to break the law, attempt to access another person's account or another company's data, interfere with the app's operation, or upload malicious files. We may suspend accounts that do.

7. Documents

Documents your Servicer shares with you are provided for your reference. Keep your own copies of anything important. Availability of a document in the app is not a guarantee of its accuracy or completeness.

8. Deleting your account

You may delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete account, or at porchpay/delete-account. Deleting your app account does not cancel your loan, erase what you owe, or end your obligations under your contract. Your Servicer retains loan records as required by law.

If the app is unavailable when a payment is due, you remain responsible for paying by another method your Servicer accepts.
