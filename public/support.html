How can we help?

Both app stores require a working support URL. Replace the contact details below with yours before submitting.

Questions about your loan, balance, or a payment

Your loan is serviced by the company that gave you your login. The fastest way to reach them is the Messages tab in the app — they see it right away and it keeps a record of the conversation.

Trouble signing in

Your Servicer issues your password. If you're locked out or never received a temporary password, message your Servicer or call them directly and ask for a password reset.

A payment isn't showing up

  • Card or Cash App Pay — usually posts within minutes.
  • Bank transfer (ACH) — can take 3–5 business days to clear and post.
  • Cash at a store — posts once the retailer reports it, usually within one business day. Keep your receipt until you see it in the app.

If a payment is still missing after that window, message your Servicer with the date, amount, and confirmation or receipt number.

Managing your data

  • Download your data — in the app, Settings → My data.
  • Delete your account — in the app, Settings → Delete account, or at this page.
  • Turn off location sharing — in the app, Loan → Location sharing. This deletes your location history.
