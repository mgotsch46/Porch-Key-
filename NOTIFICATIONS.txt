NOTIFICATIONS — how they work and what you need to do
=====================================================

Three separate things, and they behave differently. Worth knowing which is which.


1. IN-APP BADGES  — works everywhere, nothing to set up
------------------------------------------------------
Red count bubbles on the buyer's Pay, Docs and Messages tabs. Appears the moment
something happens; clears when they open that tab. Works in any browser, installed
or not. Nothing can block it.


2. APP ICON BADGE — the number on the home screen icon
------------------------------------------------------
Requires the app to be installed to the home screen. Works on Android and desktop
Chrome/Edge. On iPhone it needs iOS 16.4+ AND notification permission granted.
Shows the total unread count across all categories.


3. POP-UP NOTIFICATIONS — arrive when the app is closed
-------------------------------------------------------
This is real push. The buyer turns it on under Settings -> Notifications, and can
switch individual categories off (messages, documents, payment reminders, past-due
alerts, receipts).

  Android Chrome     works in a browser tab or installed
  Desktop Chrome     works
  iPhone / iPad      ONLY if the buyer adds the app to their home screen first.
                     Safari in a normal tab cannot receive push. That is Apple's
                     restriction, not something the app can work around.

Nothing you do depends on push succeeding — the badge and the in-app feed always
record it either way.


WHAT TRIGGERS A NOTIFICATION
----------------------------
  Buyer gets one when:
    - you send them a message
    - you share a document into one of their folders
    - a payment reminder fires (see below)
    - a payment posts to their loan
    - a late or legal notice goes out

  You get one when:
    - a buyer sends you a message


PAYMENT REMINDERS — the Reminders page
--------------------------------------
Three come set up already, timed against each buyer's own due date:

    5 days before   pop-up only
    On the due date pop-up + in-app message
    5 days after    pop-up + in-app message

Every part is yours to change. For each reminder you set:

    Name             what you call it
    When it fires    anywhere from 14 days before to 30 days after the due date
    How it reaches   pop-up only / in-app message only / both
    Only if unpaid   skip buyers who already paid, or send regardless
    Title and text   with merge fields — {{first_name}}, {{amount_due}},
                     {{due_date}}, {{monthly_payment}}, {{late_fee}} and more

Add as many as you like. Switch any off without deleting it.

They run on their own several times a day. "Run the reminder sweep" fires anything
due today immediately — useful for testing. A reminder never sends twice for the
same due date, so running it repeatedly is safe.

To nudge one buyer right now: open their loan -> "Send reminder". Pick a saved
reminder or type your own. It goes straight to their phone.


MESSAGE TEMPLATES
-----------------
You said you couldn't find these — they were on their own page with no route in
from Messages. Fixed. Messages now has "Manage templates" and "Payment reminders"
buttons at the top, and inside a conversation there are "Use a template" and
"Send a reminder" buttons under the composer.

Six templates ship ready: welcome, payment reminder, receipt, friendly past-due,
escrow update, payoff. Preview any of them against a real buyer before sending.


EMOJI
-----
The 🙂 button beside the message box opens a picker grouped into Friendly, Money,
Home and Paperwork. The same picker sits under the template editor and the reminder
editor, so emoji work in saved templates too.


NOTHING TO CONFIGURE
--------------------
Push signing keys generate themselves on first run and persist. If you ever want to
pin them yourself, set VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY and VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY in Railway.


ONE THING WORTH TELLING BUYERS
------------------------------
iPhone users only get pop-ups after adding the app to their home screen. Worth a
line in your welcome message: "Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, so you get
payment reminders." The welcome template is a good place for it.
